Date: 2024-03-01

---

- **Purpose**: The blog page serves as a platform for sharing experiences, difficulties, and insights to help others, particularly those in college or starting their college journey. The main goal is to prevent others from making the same mistakes and to facilitate learning from past experiences.

---
- **Content Overview**:
  1. **Sharing Experiences**: Includes Medium posts sharing personal experiences and reflections.
  2. **Useful Softwares**: Provides information on the most useful software tools and how to use them productively.
  3. **Mistakes Made and Lessons Learned**: Reflects on mistakes made and the lessons learned from them.
  4. **Mentorship**: Offers mentorship guidance, particularly tailored for students in tier 3 colleges and beyond.
  5. **Resources for Computer Science Students**: Offers resources and insights for computer science students, including the integration of AI into daily life.
