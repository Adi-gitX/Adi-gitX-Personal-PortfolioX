I started my journey of contributing to open source projects by choosing the organization OpenCV. Although I faced challenges and struggled with some pull requests, I persevered and worked on several issues. Eventually, I decided to switch to another organization called CC-Commens, where I found more success. I connected with mentors, resolved issues, and even created some of my own. This organization became my final choice.

I submitted my GSoC proposal, hoping for acceptance. Despite making fewer contributions than I initially planned, this experience marks the beginning of my journey. I've learned valuable lessons and plan to contribute more next year, aiming to clear GSoC by overcoming this year's challenges. This goal is now part of my "2025 todo list."
