I joined Newton School Of Technology for B.Tech in CS and AI, and I got my new MacBook Pro M2. After learning all the basic to advanced level commands, I installed several useful apps. Here are the most useful ones:

1. Homebrew: Helps install useful software and apps using just the terminal.
2. iTerm: A powerful terminal alternative.
3. VPN Pro: Used for VPN connections.
4. Amphetamine: Keeps the screen awake when needed.
5. Notion/Obsidian: Note-taking apps.
